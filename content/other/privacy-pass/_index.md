{{< hint type=tip >}}
Privacy Pass is available as a browser extension, has been implemented by Cloudflare, Apple, and others, and is an official IETF standard ([RFC 9576](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc9576/), [RFC 9577](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc9577/), [RFC 9578](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc9578/)).
{{</hint>}}


- **Proposed by** Alex Davidson (Royal Holloway, University of London (work completed during an internship at Cloudflare)), Ian Goldberg (University of Waterloo), Nick Sullivan (Cloudflare), George Tankersley, Filippo Valsorda
- [Initial Website](https://privacypass.github.io/)
- [IETF Working Group](https://github.com/ietf-wg-privacypass)
- [Cloudflare Research Website](https://research.cloudflare.com/projects/application-privacy/privacy-pass/)
