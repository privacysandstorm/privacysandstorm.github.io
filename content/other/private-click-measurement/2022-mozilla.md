**Title:** [An Analysis of Apple’s Private Click Measurement](https://mozilla.github.io/ppa-docs/pcm.pdf)

**Authors:** Martin Thomson (Mozilla)

**Abstract/Summary:** Apple has proposed Private Click Measurement (PCM) as a means of allowing websites to measure the effect of advertising. A privacy analysis of PCM is provided, examining its claims in terms of what privacy assurances it provides. We also include a discussion of the impact of the privacy protections in PCM on its usefulness for measuring advertising
