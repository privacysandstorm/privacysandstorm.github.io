**Title:** [Privacy And Competition Concerns with Google’s Privacy Sandbox](https://brave.com/web-standards-at-brave/6-privacy-sandbox-concerns/)

**Authors:** Peter Snyder (Brave)

**Abstract/Summary:** We present our concerns with Privacy Sandbox not only as a browser maker, but as individuals worried that Privacy Sandbox threatens what makes the Web special and unique: that users can modify their Web experience to best suit their needs and wants, and that features in the Web are designed first and foremost to benefit users.
