**Title:** [Reconciling Privacy Sandbox initiatives with EU data protection laws.](https://lukaszolejnik.com/stuff/PrivacySandbox_PAAPI_LLM_LO.pdf)

**Authors:** Lukasz Olejnik (The University of Edinburgh)

**Abstract/Summary:** In this dissertation, I consider a complex Privacy Sandbox proposal, the Protected Audience API (PAA). The measure is designed to reach audiences with content/ads in privacy-improved ways. To appreciate the changes, the analysis must be firmly anchored in the context of data protection
