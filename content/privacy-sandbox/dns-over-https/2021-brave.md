**Title:** [Encrypting DNS Zone Transfers ](https://brave.com/web-standards-at-brave/5-encrypting-dns-zone-transfers/)

**Authors:** Shivan Kaul Sahib (Brave), Pete Snyder (brave)

**Abstract/Summary:** Brave, along with a team of DNS experts from the industry and open source communities, recently helped publish an IETF standard (RFC 9103) to fix a long-standing privacy and security hole in the DNS.
