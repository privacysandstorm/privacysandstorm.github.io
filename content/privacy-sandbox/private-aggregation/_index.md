## Overview

The Private Aggregation API is similar to the Attribution Reporting API, but is meant to measure more general single and cross-site events, supporting other Privacy Sandbox APIs, such as Shared Storage or Protected Audience.

- [Documentation (Android)](https://developers.google.com/privacy-sandbox/relevance/private-aggregation)
- [Explainer](https://github.com/patcg-individual-drafts/private-aggregation-api)
