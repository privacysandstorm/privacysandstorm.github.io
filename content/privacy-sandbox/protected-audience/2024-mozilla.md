**Title:** [Protected Audience Privacy Analysis](https://mozilla.github.io/ppa-docs/protected-audience.pdf)

**Authors:** Martin Thomson (Mozilla)

**Abstract/Summary:** Google’s Privacy Sandbox includes a number of advertising features. The most ambitious of these is Protected Audience. This proposal aims to support the use of cross-site information about user activity in targeted advertising without enabling the collection of
that information. This analysis looks at whether Protected Audience achieves the privacy objectives it sets.

**Other:** [Blog post](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/privacy-security/googles-protected-audience-protects-advertisers/)
